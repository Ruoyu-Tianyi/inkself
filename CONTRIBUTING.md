# Contributions

Discuss substantial contributions in an issue first. The project retains the ability to offer commercial licenses for its own code.

Before accepting external code, the maintainer must obtain an explicit written contributor agreement allowing distribution under the project license and sublicensing under separate commercial terms. Contributors retain ownership. Opening a pull request alone does not assign copyright or grant additional relicensing rights. External code must not be merged until that agreement is in place.

Identify third-party material and preserve its notices. Contribute only material you are authorized to license. Do not submit personal handwriting, private documents, model weights or datasets.
